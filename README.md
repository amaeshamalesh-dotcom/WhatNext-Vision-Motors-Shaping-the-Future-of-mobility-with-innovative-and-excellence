# WhatNext Vision Motors

**Shaping the Future of Mobility with Innovation and Excellence**

## Project Overview

WhatNext Vision Motors is a Salesforce-based automotive management project designed to streamline vehicle, customer, sales, and service operations through a centralized CRM platform.

## Objectives

* Manage vehicle and customer information efficiently
* Track vehicle sales and customer transactions
* Maintain service and maintenance records
* Automate business processes using Salesforce
* Improve data management and operational efficiency

## Salesforce Features Used

* Custom Objects and Fields
* Salesforce Lightning
* Record Management
* Flows and Automation
* Reports and Dashboards
* Data Management
* Security and Access Controls

## Key Features

* Vehicle Management
* Customer Management
* Sales Management
* Service Management
* Automated Workflows
* Reports and Dashboards

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how Salesforce can be customized to create an efficient automotive management solution while improving data organization, process automation, and customer management.

## Technology

* **Platform:** Salesforce CRM
* **Interface:** Salesforce Lightning
* **Automation:** Salesforce Flow
* **Reporting:** Salesforce Reports and Dashboards
